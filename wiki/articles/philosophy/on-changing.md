# On Changing

It is around 6pm in [[Dubai]] and the sun is still brutal.

I am sitting on my balcony because I thought I should spend some time doing nothing. Or at least nothing useful. Just sit here for a while and see what comes up.

The table was dirty, so I wiped it down. That reminded me of an old towel I brought from [[Vietnam]] when I moved here. There was nothing special about it. It was not beautiful or sentimental. I kept using it because it was there.

For almost a year, that towel followed me around this apartment. I finally threw it away recently. It came all the way from a small town in northern Vietnam and somehow ended up here in Dubai.

So did I.

## Things that travel quietly

I did not think much about throwing the towel away at the time. Now I am wondering what else I carried here without noticing.

I still care about what certain people think of me, including people I barely speak to anymore. Their opinions probably belong to an older version of my life. Maybe they belong to an older version of me too. Somehow they still get a vote.

Physical things are easier. A towel gets old, I put it in the bin, and the apartment has one less object in it. There is no bin for an identity that has stopped fitting. The mind keeps old furniture.

Two years ago, I do not think I could have pictured this: me on a balcony in Dubai, looking at a life that would have seemed very far from where I grew up. The outside of my life has moved quickly. Some part of me is still crossing the distance.

## The sunset I keep missing

The sun is starting to set now.

Back in Vietnam, I used to notice sunsets. I would actually stop and look at them. Maybe because they did not always appear like this. Here the sky is clear almost every day. The sun is bright, perfectly round, impossible to miss.

And somehow I miss it all the time.

Maybe seeing something every day is one way of becoming blind to it. Today I have been sitting here long enough to notice it again.

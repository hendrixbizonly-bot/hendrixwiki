# Armaan

Armaan is an entrepreneur and the founder of Revor. He is also one of my very good friends, which is the more important fact here.

## Extremely serious life coaching

He has life-coached me before, at least according to us. The sessions were funny enough that I should be careful about giving them too much authority, but some useful things probably slipped through anyway.

Our senses of humor match quite well. That gives the friendship an easy rhythm. We can move from an actual conversation about life into making fun of the conversation before either of us has time to become too self-important.

## The group around him

We play padel and football together, often with [[Shaurya]] and [[Aswathy]]. Armaan has an energy that makes people around him feel comfortable. It is not forced charm. He is caring, and the care survives the jokes.

I respect him as a friend. I can say that without making the page sound like his LinkedIn profile or a dramatic tribute. He is someone I am glad is in my life, and someone with whom ordinary time is reliably good.

# Nmy

Nmy is a Vietnamese architect and one of my online friends. A lot of our friendship lives in conversation: film, design, aesthetics, shape, visual culture, and then life when the conversation drifts there.

We have similar taste in film and television. *[[Game of Thrones]]* is one obvious overlap, but the more interesting thing is the way we talk about what we watch. A frame can lead into color, a building, a costume, or why one fictional world feels inhabited while another feels assembled.

## Probably Late

She helped me think about and design the camera filters for [[Probably Late]]. Her architectural eye shows up in the way she notices proportion and shape, but she also has a softer artistic instinct that is useful for a camera product. A filter is technical until it reaches an image. Then taste takes over.

I find her kind, thoughtful, and artistic. She is easy to talk to without the conversation becoming empty.

We have talked and joked about going hiking someday when I am back in [[Vietnam]]. Maybe we actually will haha.

# Aswathy

## The energy around her

Aswathy is one of those people who seems to arrive with more energy than the room had before. She runs her family business in oil and gas and is also involved with Loki, an app for finding things to do in [[Dubai]]. Both make sense when I think about how much motion she can hold at once.

She is very warm, socially mature, caring, and kind. She notices other people. I like that about her more than any list of things she does.

I genuinely enjoy being around her and would like us to become good friends. That is the simple version.

## Annoyingly athletic

We have played padel and football and gone [[Running]] together. She is very good at both sports, in the irritating way that makes me suspect she would pick up tennis and immediately be good at that too.

I sometimes call the whole combination her “Type A baddie” energy. It is intentionally a little unserious, but it is accurate. She tries hard without making effort feel miserable. Sometimes seeing how seriously she shows up makes me want to push myself harder too.

## The Odyssey Challenge

We started something called [[The Odyssey Challenge]]. The rough idea is to reduce unnecessary comfort and deliberately pursue excellence and consistency. There is a shared album around it, which makes the challenge feel partly like training and partly like a record of a period.

We also watched *The Odyssey* together. The name became less abstract after that.

I enjoy doing it. The mix of shared activities and documenting them has also found its way into how I think about [[Probably Late]], especially the idea that a collection of moments can belong to the people inside it without becoming a public performance.

Swift is part of my technical skill set for building native applications in Apple's ecosystem.

I came to it from a broader mix of product design, application development, and JavaScript-based work. That makes the comparison useful. JavaScript gives me range across the web and different parts of a product stack. Swift gives me a more direct route into native Apple applications.

I do not want to inflate the label beyond the work. This page records Swift as a real working skill and an area I can keep developing, not a claim that I have exhausted the language.
